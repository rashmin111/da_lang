The First Riddle
The screen turned blue. Words began to appear, one letter at a time. It looked like the clock was typing.
"Riddle One: I am full of numbers, but I am not a book. I live on the wall and help you look. What am I?"
Tom read the riddle out loud. He was quiet for a moment, thinking hard. Then he smiled. "A clock! It’s a clock, like you!"
The clock made a happy ding sound. "Correct," it said. "You are smart. One step at a time. Keep going."
Tom nodded. "What happens now?"
The screen showed a small map. A red dot blinked. Under it, a message said: "Look under the couch."
Tom got out of bed. The house was still and dark. He walked to the living room and looked under the couch. Something shiny caught his eye.
It was a small box with a golden ring tied to it with string. He picked it up and opened it. Inside was a soft feather and a card.
The card said, "For the brave and kind."
Tom smiled proudly. He felt excited. This was not just a game. It was something special. Something magic.
He went back to his room and placed the feather on the clock.
"Good job," said the voice. "Tomorrow at midnight, we continue."
Tom nodded. He lay back in bed, holding the box. He couldn’t wait for the next riddle.
